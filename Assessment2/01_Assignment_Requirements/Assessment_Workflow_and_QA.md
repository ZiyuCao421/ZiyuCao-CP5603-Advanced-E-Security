# Assessment Workflow and Quality Check

## Sequence

1. Create the master evidence inventory.
2. Record observed facts and evidence boundaries.
3. Build evidence chains and identify findings.
4. Classify findings: threat, vulnerability, control failure, or process issue.
5. Form each risk as asset/process + threat + gap → risk event → impact.
6. Score inherent risk, evaluate controls, and record residual risk.
7. Collect external risk intelligence separately from the Evidence Pack.
8. Apply ACS ethics to decisions and trade-offs.
9. Select final risks, treatments, owners, and roadmap actions.
10. Quality-check traceability before drafting the report.

## Quality checks

- [ ] Each factual claim has an Evidence ID.
- [ ] Each interpretation records its limitation or alternative explanation.
- [ ] Policies, approvals, and alerts are not treated as proof of operating controls, deployment, or compromise.
- [ ] Every priority has likelihood, impact, evidence, and a clear rationale.
- [ ] Implemented, planned, partial, and unknown controls are distinguished.
- [ ] External research supports analysis but does not replace case evidence.
- [ ] ACS ethics are tied to specific decisions.
- [ ] No original evidence, raw logs, personal data, credentials, or sensitive technical detail is placed in this public repository.
